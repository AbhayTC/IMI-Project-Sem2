================================================================================
PROJECT: AI-DRIVEN INVERSE DESIGN OF SPACE-GRADE POLYMERS
================================================================================

1. PROBLEM STATEMENT & OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Problem: The development of space-grade materials is currently slow and 
    expensive due to a reliance on extensive physical vacuum testing. There is 
    no efficient way to predict performance directly from chemical structures.
* Objective: To utilize a high-dimensional QSPR (Quantitative Structure-Property 
    Relationship) approach to understand how a material’s structure affects its 
    thermal endurance and dielectric stability.
* Goal: Enable faster, efficient digital screening of high-performance 
    polymers (Polyimide, PEEK, PTFE) for satellite protection.

2. DATA REPRESENTATION & FEATURE ENGINEERING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Total Features: 120 numerical inputs per polymer sample.
* Feature Groups:
    * Structural (1-40): RDKit-derived counts (e.g., rings, molecular weight).
    * Contextual (41-80): polyBERT embeddings for chemical "context."
    * Physics/Morphological (81-120): Gaussian sampling from historical 
        priors (Crystallinity, Free Volume Fraction, Modulus).
* Molecular Fingerprinting:
    * Type: ECFP4 (Extended-Connectivity Fingerprints).
    * Radius: 2 (Diameter: 4).
    * Format: 64-bit vector used for Tanimoto Similarity comparisons.

3. MODEL ARCHITECTURE (THE QSPR ENGINE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Model Type: Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) Regressor.
* Topology: Funnel architecture [256 -> 128 -> 64] hidden nodes.
* Activation (ReLU): Rectified Linear Unit allows the model to learn 
    non-linear patterns and threshold-based chemical behaviors.
* Optimizer (Adam): Adaptive Moment Estimation. The "teacher" that minimizes 
    error during training by adjusting weights based on prediction loss.
* Outputs: 4 simultaneous regression predictions (Tg, Dk, Outgassing, Radiation).

4. QUALITY CONTROL & INTEGRITY (TASK 4)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Feature Leakage Detection: 
    * Scans features for "Identity Labels" (constant values per class).
    * Calculation: If Within-Class Standard Deviation < 0.15, feature is 
        flagged as "leaky" and removed.
    * Purpose: Prevents the AI from "memorizing" polymer names instead of 
        learning the actual physics (avoids Overfitting).
* Confidence Scoring: 
    * A final 0-100 metric generated by Task 4.
    * Combines R-squared (R2) training scores, physical validity checks, and 
        inverse design success rates.

5. INVERSE DESIGN (TASK 3: INVENTION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Process: Structural Mutation of base SMILES (Polyimide, PEEK, PTFE).
* Chemical Reality Filter (Tanimoto Similarity):
    * Floor (>= 0.60): Ensures the new molecule is manufacturable and stays 
        within the same polymer family.
    * Ceiling (< 0.99): Ensures the AI actually changed the structure rather 
        than just copying the original.
* Output: A "candidates.csv" shopping list of new, high-performance 
    molecules that hit specific space-grade targets (e.g., Lower Dk, High Tg).

6. EVALUATION METRICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Regression Logic: Unlike classification, we do not use True/False Positives.
* R-Squared (R2): Measures the "Logic Grade" (how much chemistry the AI 
    actually understands).
* RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error): Measures the average "Numerical Mistake" 
    in degrees Celsius or dielectric units.
